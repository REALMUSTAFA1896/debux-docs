# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Personal, savings and society accounts, with per-role withdrawal limits on society accounts
- Cards with PINs, three-try lockout, freeze, shop payments and ATM toggles, and tiers gated by credit
- PIN lock screen on every open — no account data leaves the server until the PIN is verified
- Invoices any on-duty service job can issue, with dispute and automatic collection after the due date
- Loans in three tiers gated by a live credit score, with instalments and early settlement
- ATM panel: withdraw, deposit, send money, reset PIN
- Ten languages, one locale file per language covering both notifications and the interface
- Embedded fonts, no CDN at runtime
- Renewed-Banking compatible exports
